The vision

Plant science knows more than any one of us can read.

Its knowledge is scattered across millions of papers, images and specialised databases, and it grows every year. A researcher asking what is known about a gene, a breeder narrowing a trait interval and a student learning to weigh evidence still assemble that picture by hand. PlantScience.si brings it together in one place.

Four new capabilities

From answers in text to evidence you can see, query, teach with and trace through evolution.

Visual evidence

See the image behind the finding

Much of plant science's primary evidence is visual. Phenotype photographs, micrographs and plots from the open literature are linked to the genes, tissues and findings they show, so an answer can display its evidence, with author, journal and licence.

Database integration

Live records, never invented identifiers

Language models invent identifiers and numbers. The agent instead calls skills that query established plant bioinformatics databases directly, and every statement is labelled as literature-supported or database-supported, with identifier, release and link.

Education

A teaching mode that asks, not answers for you

Developed with educators and students. Instead of handing over an answer, the agent poses a question at the student’s level. The student searches the published findings, figures and data, then reaches their own conclusion, with safeguards against outsourcing assessed work.

Evolutionary insight

From Arabidopsis to crops, through shared ancestry

Most functional evidence comes from model species, while decisions are made in crops. A gene orthology network links each gene family across plant genomes, showing which functions are conserved, which have diverged and where evidence is missing.

In use

One question. Four kinds of evidence.

A plant scientist and a wheat breeder ask about the same gene, and each can follow every statement back to its source. In Education mode the AI asks the question, and the student finds the evidence.

How it earns trust

It synthesises what is known. Scientists stay responsible for judgement.

Traceable

Every statement links to the publication or database record it came from.

Labelled

Literature-supported and database-supported claims are marked as such.

Honest about gaps

Inferences across species are hypotheses with confidence levels. Missing evidence is shown as missing.

Open

Versioned, FAIR releases, an open benchmark, and an API any AI agent can use.

Plant Science AI Initiative

A resource built and steered by its community.

Plant science researchers, breeders, educators and database providers unite around one open intelligence resource.

  • Contribute skills. Database providers add their resources through an open specification.
  • Set the standard. Members submit and review benchmark questions, published every year.
  • Guide priorities. A public roadmap, ranked by the people who use it.
  • Build on it. Open API and Model Context Protocol endpoints, with versioned knowledge-graph releases.
